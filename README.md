# Share Me - Because Food Deserves Better!

*A little app with a big intention hehe* 

The 13 year old me built **Share Me** with a simple thought... why should perfectly good food go to waste when it could mean so much to someone else?

The idea was to create a platform where people and restaurants could **donate or sell their surplus food**, helping reduce food wastage while making sharing a little easier.

### 🍱 How It Works
- Sign in and choose whether to **donate or sell** food.
- Enter details about the food, its quantity, and freshness.
- Complete a **food quality self-assessment** to encourage safe sharing.
- Provide your location for a proposed food pickup service.

### 💻 Built With
**JavaScript, p5.js, and HTML**

This project helped me explore interactive forms, user inputs, buttons, and navigating between different screens using JavaScript.

### 💌 Why I Made This

Looking back, I love that I was already thinking about how technology could solve everyday problems. It wasn't just about building something that worked, but about building something that could actually help people.

This was an early prototype, and not all the proposed services were fully implemented, but the idea behind it still makes me smile.
